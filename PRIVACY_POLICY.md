## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how **Elementa** ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by **PoshDev**, handles user information.

Your privacy is paramount. Elementa is engineered from the ground up as a **100% offline, privacy-first educational and reference tool**. We do not collect, harvest, store, transmit, monetize, or share any personal information, usage metrics, or device identifiers.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Data
**We do not collect any personal data.**  
Elementa does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or credentials.

### B. Element Searches, Filters & Browsing Data
All interactions within the App — including searching for elements by symbol or name, zooming and panning the periodic table grid, applying category filters, and viewing element properties (such as atomic mass, electron configurations, and group/period data) — happen **strictly on your local device in real-time**. Your search queries, browsing history, and interactions are never logged, stored, or transmitted to any external server.

### C. Device and Technical Information
**We do not collect device information or telemetry.**  
The App does not capture your IP address, Android ID, Advertising ID (GAID), MAC address, hardware specifications, or operating system identifiers.

---

## 3. Device Permissions & Network Access

* **Zero Internet Permissions:** Elementa does not declare or use the `android.permission.INTERNET` permission in its application manifest.
* **100% Offline Architecture:** The entire dataset of 118 chemical elements is bundled directly within the App installation (`assets/data/elements.json`). The App operates fully without an active internet connection and is completely functional in Airplane Mode. It makes no remote API calls, network handshakes, or server requests.
* **Sensitive Permissions:** The App does not request access to your device storage, camera, microphone, contacts, location, sensors, or media files.

---

## 4. Local Data Storage

* **Offline Dataset:** All chemical and physical element properties are stored locally as static, read-only assets bundled with the App.
* **App Preferences:** If the App saves user preferences (such as theme selection or display options), they are stored locally on your device using standard local platform preferences. This data remains on your physical hardware, is never transmitted externally, and is deleted when you clear app data or uninstall the application.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

* **No Analytics:** We do not integrate Google Analytics, Firebase, Sentry, Mixpanel, or any telemetry or error-tracking SDKs.
* **No Advertisements:** Elementa is completely ad-free. It does not integrate Google AdMob, Unity Ads, or any third-party advertising or profiling frameworks.
* **No Third-Party SDKs:** The application does not embed social login plugins, marketing trackers, or third-party web views.

---

## 6. Children’s Privacy

Because Elementa is an educational chemistry reference tool that collects absolutely no personal data from any user, it is completely safe for all audiences, including children under the age of 13. Elementa fully complies with the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**.

---

## 7. Data Security

Because Elementa operates entirely offline and does not collect or transmit personal information across networks or store data on cloud servers, your information is inherently safeguarded against remote vulnerabilities, data leaks, and unauthorized access.
